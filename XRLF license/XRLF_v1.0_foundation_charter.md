# XRLF Protocol Foundation Charter Draft

XRLF Protocol Foundation - Charter Draft

## 
1. Name
XRLF Protocol Foundation ("The Foundation")

## 
2. Purpose
The XRLF Protocol Foundation exists to:
- Steward the XRLF cognitive model format and runtime protocol
- Maintain open, stable, and community-accessible standards
- Ensure long-term interoperability across implementations
- Protect the XRLF name, specification, and compliance rules
- Manage commercial licensing for enterprise and cloud-scale use
- Support the open-source community through permissive licensing
- Encourage research, experimentation, and innovation in reasoning systems The Foundation is non-profit in spirit, even if legally simple at first.

## 
3. Scope of Authority
The Foundation governs:
- The XRLF specification (v1.0, v1.1, v2.0, etc.)
- The XRLF container format
- The XRLF runtime protocol
- The XRLF adapter API
- The XRLF multimodal gateway rules
- The XRLF compliance test suite
- The XRLF trademark and branding
- The XRLF commercial licensing model The Foundation does not govern:
- Any specific model weights
- Any specific implementation of XRLF

 Any proprietary extensions created by third parties XRLF remains open and extensible.

## 
4. Governance Model
The Foundation uses a single-founder governance model with optional advisors.

## 
4.1 Founder
Daniel Holds final authority over:
- Specification changes
- Licensing terms
- Compliance rules
- Foundation direction

## 
4.2 Advisors (Optional)
Advisors may be added to:
- Review specification drafts
- Suggest improvements
- Provide technical insight
- Represent community interests Advisors do not have binding authority.

## 
5. Membership
Membership is optional and free. Members may:
- Participate in discussions
- Propose improvements
- Submit adapter modules
- Contribute to the spec
- Help maintain compliance tests Membership does not grant voting rights. This keeps governance simple and avoids bureaucracy.

## 
7. Licensing Authority
The Foundation manages:
- Apache 2.0 base license
- XRLF Commercial Use Exception
- Commercial licensing tiers
- OEM and cloud provider agreements The Foundation ensures:
- XRLF remains free for individuals, researchers, and small companies
- Enterprises contribute financially to sustainability
- Licensing remains simple and fair

## 
8. Trademark
The Foundation owns:  "XRLF"  "XRLF Protocol"  "XRLF Cognitive Runtime" Usage rules:
- Free for open-source projects
- Free for research
- Free for non-commercial use

 Requires commercial license for enterprise use

## 
9. Compliance
The Foundation maintains:
- XRLF compliance tests
- Adapter validation rules
- Runtime behavior requirements
- Container format correctness tests Compliance ensures interoperability across implementations.

## 
10. Roadmap Responsibility
The Foundation defines the official roadmap:  v1.0 - current  v1.1 - video, memory routing, extended adapters  v2.0 - XRLF-native multimodal model The roadmap is public and open to community feedback.

## 
11. Amendments
The Founder may amend this charter at any time. Amendments are published publicly.
