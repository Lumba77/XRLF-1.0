# XRLF Commercial License Tiers

XRLF Commercial License Tiers

## 
1. Purpose
The XRLF Commercial License Tiers define the pricing and usage rights for companies whose annual revenue meets or exceeds 500,000, as required by the XRLF Commercial Use Exception. These tiers are designed to maximize adoption while providing sustainable financial support for the XRLF Protocol Foundation. The pricing is intentionally conservative, accessible, and community-friendly.

## 
2. Free Tiers
These tiers require no payment and no approval.

## 
2.1 Indie License - 0
For individuals, hobbyists, students, and solo developers. Rights include:
- Full use of XRLF
- Commercial use allowed
- Redistribution allowed
- Adapter development allowed
- Branding allowed (non-enterprise context) No reporting or registration required.

## 
2.2 Startup License - 0
For startups with annual revenue under 500,

## 000. Rights include:
- Full use of XRLF in production
- Integration into commercial products
- Redistribution of XRLF-compliant runtimes
- Use of XRLF branding
- Access to compliance suite No payment required.

3. Paid Tiers
These tiers apply to companies with annual revenue equal to or exceeding 500,

## 000. The pricing is intentionally low to encourage adoption.

3.1 Small Business License - 300/year
For companies with 500,000-2,000,000 annual revenue. Rights include:
- Production use
- Redistribution of XRLF-compliant runtimes
- Integration into proprietary systems
- Use of XRLF branding
- Access to compliance suite
- Priority support for specification questions This tier is designed to be affordable for small companies.

3.2 Medium Business License - 1,000/year
For companies with 2,000,000-10,000,000 annual revenue. Rights include:
- All Small Business rights
- OEM distribution rights
- Internal XRLF training materials
- Adapter certification
- Early access to XRLF v1.1 drafts This tier supports companies scaling XRLF internally.

3.3 Enterprise License - 5,000/year
For companies with 10,000,000-100,000,000 annual revenue. Rights include:
- All Medium Business rights
- Unlimited redistribution
- Unlimited OEM integration
- XRLF branding rights
- XRLF compliance certification

 Participation in roadmap discussions
- Advisory seat eligibility This tier is designed for large-scale enterprise deployments.

3.4 OEM License - 10,000/year
For companies that:
- Embed XRLF in hardware
- Bundle XRLF with devices
- Sell XRLF-powered appliances
- Integrate XRLF into robotics or IoT Rights include:
- All Enterprise rights
- Hardware bundling rights
- XRLF trademark usage on devices
- Adapter certification for embedded systems

3.5 Cloud Provider License - 25,000/year
For companies offering XRLF-powered cloud services, including:
- XRLF inference endpoints
- XRLF multimodal gateways
- XRLF container hosting
- XRLF adapter marketplaces Rights include:
- All OEM rights
- Cloud distribution rights
- XRLF trademark usage in cloud products
- XRLF compliance certification for cloud
- Priority review for XRLF v2.0 proposals This tier ensures sustainability while keeping XRLF accessible.

4. Payment Terms
 Annual billing

 No per-seat fees
- No usage-based fees
- No hidden costs
- No royalties
- No revenue sharing XRLF licensing is intentionally simple.

## 
5. Reporting Requirements
Only paid tiers require reporting:
- Annual revenue confirmation
- Contact information
- Product names using XRLF Indie and Startup tiers require no reporting.

## 
6. License Renewal
Licenses renew annually. Companies may upgrade tiers at any time.

## 
7. Termination
Licenses may be terminated for:
- Trademark misuse
- False revenue reporting
- Non-compliance with XRLF specification
- Unauthorized redistribution Termination does not affect open-source rights under Apache 2.0.

## 
8. Relationship to Apache 

## 2.0
These commercial tiers do not restrict the Apache 2.0 license. They only apply to enterprise-scale use as defined in the XRLF Commercial Use Exception. For full details, see the XRLF license model.
